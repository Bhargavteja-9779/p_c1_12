# TURN — the selection effect has a name in medicine (written at the time)
The prior-art check for H6c found no energy paper that measures it, but the idea is old in diagnostic medicine:
"spectrum bias" — case-control studies with healthy volunteers as controls overstate specificity (Ransohoff &
Feinstein 1978; Lijmer et al. 1999). The PreDist authors even suggest evaluating on randomly selected events
(their Limitation 2) without doing it. So our contribution is to run that check and measure the effect on two
energy benchmarks, framed with the medical concept, not to claim a new kind of bias.
