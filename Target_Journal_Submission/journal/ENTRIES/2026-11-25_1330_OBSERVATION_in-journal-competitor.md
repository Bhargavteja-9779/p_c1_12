# OBSERVATION — the target journal already has a PreDist cross-manufacturer paper (written at the time)
The quality-bar survey found van Dreven et al. 2026 in Energy and AI: PreDist, M1 and M2, M1->M2 transfer, balanced
daily windows (20-83% faulty samples) and F1 at the best threshold. We had written that nobody had tested
cross-manufacturer transfer on PreDist; that was wrong and is corrected. The same paper is also our best hook: its
protocol is exactly the kind of balanced, best-threshold evaluation our study shows to be optimistic, so re-running
it as one arm on the same data lets readers of this journal see the gap on a protocol the journal itself published.
